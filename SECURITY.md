| AI-13 | Medium   | "Is approval required?" was decided by two independent copies of the same rule (planner and executor); a change to one silently diverged from the other, and there was no single place for further conditions.                                                 | One function (`sideEffectRequiresApproval`) and one engine decision for the planner, executor and orchestrator; conditions (finance) are policy rules in the same engine.                                          | `policy/test/engine.test.ts`, `ai-runtime/test/policy-enforcement.test.ts` |

**Corrections to earlier claims.** The audit found no amount threshold in
code: the sample expense policy's "₹50,000 requires CFO approval" is prose. The

**Not claimed.** The engine bounds what a model-shaped request can _cause_; it
does not make a model trustworthy and does not solve prompt injection.
Residual risks (direct REST actions outside the engine, non-expiring
approvals, the S34 fail-open ceiling for unmapped roles, cached MCP session
context, the caller-supplied `approvalGranted` boolean, composite workflow
capabilities) are listed in TRUST_MODEL.md §9.12.

  file for high-signal credential patterns (AWS/GCP keys, private keys,

  any finding (ENGINEERING_PLAYBOOK.md §10.1).

  `pnpm audit` before pilot.
